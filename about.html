About SmRF

SmRF brings together software development, electrical engineering, and product experience to advance residential radon mitigation.

SmRF Software Development

Michael Gutmann

Mike has worked at the forefront of PC, Internet, and digital multimedia technologies for more than thirty years. During two decades at Intel Corporation, he served as a project manager, software architect, and programmer. He was a lead engineer on the Intel Videophone, which shipped in more than five million OEM PCs, and is a co-inventor on ten U.S. patents.

Following Intel, he pursued ventures that included building and operating a self-storage facility, teaching mathematics at the University of Portland for 10 years, and serving as CSO of AvidBeam Technologies. SmRF represents a return to his core interests in applied technology, product development, and practical innovation.

Mike holds a BS in Mathematics and an MS in Computer Science from the Institute of Technology at the University of Minnesota.

SmRF Hardware Development

Chris Hansen

Chris recently retired as Director of IoT Engineering at Nordic Semiconductor following a long career in wireless networking, embedded systems, and IoT technology. Before joining Nordic, he spent 15 years as a principal engineer at Intel, then 10 years in engineering leadership at Nordic.

He was a key contributor to the development and adoption of Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and served as Chairman of the Bluetooth SIG Board of Directors from 2010 to 2013. Chris brings a lifetime of electrical-engineering and product-development experience to SmRF.

He holds a BSEE from the Technical University of Denmark and an MSCE from Northeastern University.
