SmRF installation architecture

SmRF is designed to use a simple two-wire, low-voltage 0–10 V control connection to compatible variable-speed EC radon fans. Fantech and Festa are planned fan-integration targets. RadonAway may also be supported if suitable two-wire control terminals are exposed and verified for compatibility. The fan retains its conventional AC power connection.

Controller

  • Raspberry Pi controller with local automation software
  • Separate compatible continuous radon monitor providing readings over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
  • 0–10 V DAC fan-control output
  • Optional remote Viewer and reporting connectivity on SmRF WiFi

Fan connection

  • Compatible variable-speed EC radon fan with a suitable two-wire 0–10 V control input; targeted models include Fantech and Festa
  • Conventional continuous AC power to the fan
  • 18/2 low-voltage control cable from SmRF to the manufacturer's designated control terminals, subject to model-specific wiring requirements
  • No fan-side Wi-Fi control device required

SmRF Mini and SmRF WiFi product direction

Both controllers are being designed to obtain readings over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) from a separate compatible continuous radon monitor, rather than incorporating a radon sensor. Airthings is currently supported. Aranet and Ecosense CRM integration are under development. SmRF provides the target-based control algorithm and a two-wire 0–10 V command for compatible EC fans, initially targeting Fantech and Festa. RadonAway support depends on an accessible compatible interface. Specific fan models require validation. SmRF WiFi additionally supports optional remote reporting and diagnostics.

Final installation details, wiring practices, and regulatory requirements are subject to production engineering and certification.
