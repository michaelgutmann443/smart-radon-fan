SmRF installation architecture

SmRF uses a simple hardwired low-voltage path between the controller and a compatible variable-speed radon fan. The fan keeps conventional AC power; SmRF supplies the 0–10 V control command.

Controller

  • Raspberry Pi controller with local automation software
  • Separate compatible continuous radon monitor supplying feedback; final communication interface under development
  • 0–10 V DAC fan-control output
  • Optional remote Viewer and reporting connectivity on SmRF WiFi

Fan connection

  • Compatible EC variable-speed radon fan
  • Conventional continuous AC power to the fan
  • Ordinary 18/2 cable from SmRF to the fan control terminals
  • No fan-side Wi-Fi control device required

SmRF Mini and SmRF WiFi product direction

Both controllers are designed to obtain radon readings from a separate compatible continuous radon monitor, rather than incorporating a radon sensor into the controller. SmRF provides the target-based control algorithm and hardwired EC fan command. SmRF WiFi additionally supports remote reporting and diagnostics; final CRM compatibility and local interfaces are still being evaluated.

Final installation details, wiring practices, and regulatory requirements are subject to production engineering and certification.
