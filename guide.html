SmRF installation architecture

SmRF uses a simple hardwired low-voltage path between the controller and a compatible variable-speed radon fan. The fan keeps conventional AC power; SmRF supplies the 0–10 V control command.

Controller

  • Raspberry Pi controller with local automation software
  • Continuous radon monitor connected locally
  • 0–10 V DAC fan-control output
  • Remote Viewer and reporting connectivity

Fan connection

  • Compatible EC variable-speed radon fan
  • Conventional continuous AC power to the fan
  • Ordinary 18/2 cable from SmRF to the fan control terminals
  • No fan-side Wi-Fi control device required
Final installation details, wiring practices, and regulatory requirements are subject to production engineering and certification.
