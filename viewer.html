What the Viewer shows

The Viewer turns individual SmRF site reports into an operational dashboard for homeowners, installers, and support staff. It is intended to make radon mitigation performance visible instead of hidden after installation.

Why this matters

A conventional mitigation system can be difficult to evaluate after installation unless someone returns to test, inspect, or troubleshoot. SmRF Viewer adds remote visibility, making the mitigation system easier to monitor, explain, and support over time.

From installation to ongoing verification

SmRF combines fan control with remote monitoring so each site can report measured radon levels, fan behavior, and device health. The Viewer is the operational layer that makes those reports useful.
