Introducing SmRF Mini

The missing control loop in residential radon mitigation.

SmRF Mini reads a CRM to automatically adjust an EC variable-speed fan based on a specified radon target level.

Measure. Adjust. Verify. Repeat.

A complete local control loop—not an always-on fan.

Purpose-built for fan control

Closed-loop radon mitigation using a separate monitor.

SmRF is designed to work with an established continuous radon monitor over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), rather than incorporate a new radon sensor. Airthings is currently supported. Aranet and Ecosense CRM integration are under development. This approach lets us focus development on dependable control, EC fan integration, and a practical path to market.

⌁

Independent BLE radon monitor

SmRF is being developed to receive readings over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) from an independently supplied CRM, leaving sensing to established monitor manufacturers and fan control to SmRF.

◉

Variable-speed EC fans

SmRF Mini is planned to support compatible Fantech and Festa EC fan models with a two-wire 0–10 V control input, rather than being tied to one fan manufacturer. RadonAway support may follow if suitable control terminals are available and verified.

▣

Local display

See current radon, 30-day average, fan speed, fan utilization, target, and operating status.

✓

Local autonomous control

The entire measurement and control process operates inside the home without internet service.

Measured in real homes

Built from field evidence—not theory alone.

Existing SmRF prototypes have generated the operating data needed to develop and validate the variable-speed control algorithms that SmRF Mini will use.

  • Radon response measured against commanded fan output
  • Target-based stepped speed changes tested in occupied homes
  • Daily, 7-day, and 30-day performance tracked over time

One control platform, a clear product path

From autonomous control to connected fleet visibility.

SmRF Mini and SmRF WiFi are being designed to receive radon readings from a separate compatible BLE monitor. SmRF Mini focuses on local variable-speed fan control; SmRF WiFi adds connected reporting and diagnostics while keeping fan control local. SmRF Viewer provides the optional remote monitoring layer. Neither controller incorporates its own radon sensor.

SmRF Mini→SmRF WiFi→SmRF Viewer
Focused first product

SmRF Mini

An autonomous local fan controller planned to use BLE readings from a separate compatible CRM, with target selection, automatic 0–10 V fan control, and local operating status.

  • Planned Bluetooth LE monitor link
  • Local target-based control
  • Direct 0–10 V EC fan command
Connected evolution

SmRF WiFi

The connected SmRF platform is being designed to receive radon feedback from a separate compatible BLE CRM and combine local variable-speed fan control with WiFi reporting, remote diagnostics, software support, and installation visibility.

  • Control remains local
  • Remote reporting and diagnostics
  • Foundation for connected services
Remote monitoring layer

SmRF Viewer

The Viewer turns connected installations into a remotely visible fleet with performance history, operating status, attention rules, reports, and service information.

  • Installation and fleet status
