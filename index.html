How it works

A radon sensor reports indoor concentrations. SmRF collects, records, and analyzes the readings, providing both remote visibility into mitigation performance and automated control of the radon fan.

System components

  • Radon sensor providing short-term and/or long-term readings.
  • Controller (embedded platform) running the automation logic.
  • Controllable power interface (smart plug / relay) powering the fan.
  • Radon mitigation fan and vent stack as commonly installed in homes.

Installer-friendly setup

  • Simple onboarding flow accessible from a smartphone.
  • Runs locally on your network and is viewable via IP address.
  • Logs radon and fan state history for confidence and verification.

Remote monitoring and management

  • SmRF Viewer shows current radon levels, averages, fan state, and site status.
  • Installers can review logs and charts remotely to understand mitigation performance.
  • Viewer tools support future alerting, diagnostics, and service prioritization.
